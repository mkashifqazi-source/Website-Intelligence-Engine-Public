# Evidence and programme authority

Source evidence, research acceptance and implementation authorization are separate authorities. The current checkpoint permits Phase-1 research and reproducibility/governance work while leaving Phase 2 and production implementation gated.

Model-assisted research must expose actual supporting resources. Fluent text, a generated score or a corpus count is not authority. The distinction between crawler access, eligibility, retrieval, grounding, citation, referral and training permission prevents inappropriate permission or ranking claims.

Future LPI/GSC integration would cross an evidence-provider boundary. Those services have their own access controls; their existence does not grant WIE authorization to query private properties or bind its architecture to them. No production MCP, authentication platform, paid provider or tenant model is selected here.

The public case study excludes internal research prompts, rejected-run contents, unpublished corpus/reports, account details and implementation plans beyond the documented conceptual boundaries. It explains epistemic discipline without copying private research.

The long-term aim is AI-readable evidence, but observation delivery is not permission for an agent to change websites, cloud resources or accounts. Execution authority is not established by the current research phase.
