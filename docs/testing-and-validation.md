# Research validation

The current quality strategy is specified research acceptance, not a demonstrated production test suite. An accepted-signal JSON Schema is present. Executable research validators and populated-corpus acceptance results were not established in the inspected scripts/tests inventory.

Validation layers are intentionally distinct:

- Structural: required fields, types, enums and forbidden extras.
- Identity/integrity: stable IDs, reference resolution and source-state lineage.
- Semantic: atomicity, duplicate detection and claim/source alignment.
- Temporal: applicability, freshness and supersession.
- Coverage: domain/lifecycle/entity/relationship completeness and saturation.
- Acceptance: structured QC, independent audit and formal freeze.

The schema explicitly says structural validation does not establish source support, normalization, QC, saturation or audit acceptance. Primary-source support also requires a compatible source-register entry, beyond field validation.

The checkpoint records a verified web-research smoke test and continued rejection of earlier ungrounded attempts. This overview omits private account history and does not reuse failed output as evidence.

No validation scripts, external research or production tests were run for the showcase. Phase 1 remains in progress. A future implemented validator needs meaningful fixtures for unresolved references, duplicate semantic IDs, unsupported evidence classes, conflicting sources and stale platform claims.
